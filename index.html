About Computers.

Learn more about modern computer technology.

What is a computer?

A computer is an electronic device that processes data, stores information and performs different tasks.

Which computer is best for gaming?

Gaming computers with powerful processors, dedicated graphics cards and advanced cooling systems offer excellent gaming performance.

What is the difference between SSD and HDD?

SSD drives are faster and quieter, while HDD drives usually provide more storage for a lower price.

How long does a computer last?

Most computers can last around 4–7 years depending on their quality, usage and maintenance.
